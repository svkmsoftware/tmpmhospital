# Your Knee, Your Choice: How HTO Surgery Helps You Avoid Knee Replacement

Climbing stairs feels harder than it used to. Sitting down for prayer or getting up from the floor brings a twinge of pain you'd rather not admit to. If this sounds familiar, you're not alone — knee pain quietly creeps into the lives of millions of people once they cross 40.

And if a doctor has already mentioned the words "**Total Knee Replacement**," it's natural to feel a little overwhelmed. Before you accept that as the only path forward, there's something worth knowing:

**In many cases, your own natural knee can be saved.**

That's exactly what **High Tibial Osteotomy (HTO)** — a knee preservation surgery — is designed to do.

## So, What Exactly Is HTO?

Think of HTO as a "realignment" surgery rather than a "replacement" one. It's used when only one side of your knee's cartilage has worn down — not the whole joint. Instead of removing the joint and putting in an artificial one, the surgeon gently corrects the angle of your shin bone (tibia) so your body weight shifts away from the damaged part of the knee and onto the healthier side.

Less pressure on the worn area means less pain — and a joint that's still entirely, naturally yours.

## Is HTO Right for You?

This isn't a one-size-fits-all surgery, but it tends to be an excellent fit for people who are:

- **Between 40 and 70 years old** and still active — perhaps too young to feel ready for a full knee replacement, or simply keen to hold on to their natural joint a while longer.
- **Dealing with wear on just one side** of the knee, rather than widespread arthritis across the whole joint.
- **Noticeably bow-legged**, which puts uneven, extra strain on one side of the knee with every step.

A quick consultation and X-ray with an experienced orthopedic surgeon can tell you whether you fit this picture.

## Why Patients Choose HTO

- **Keep what's yours.** No artificial implant — your natural knee joint stays intact.
- **Buy yourself time.** HTO can push back the need for a total knee replacement by **10 to 15 years**.
- **Get your life back.** After a full recovery — and with the right physiotherapy — many patients can once again sit cross-legged for prayer, get up and down off the floor comfortably, and use an Indian-style toilet without discomfort. These are everyday movements that a total knee replacement often restricts.

## The Bottom Line

A diagnosis doesn't have to feel like a dead end. If you're between 40 and 70 and knee pain is holding you back, take a breath, and talk to an experienced orthopedic surgeon about whether HTO could work for you. Made at the right time, this decision could mean many more pain-free years on your own two knees.
